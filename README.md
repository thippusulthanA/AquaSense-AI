# 👑 OmniAqua AI — Unified Enterprise Master Agent Platform

OmniAqua AI is a commercial-grade, single unified Master AI Agent platform combining **all capabilities across 8 specialized AI Sub-Agents** from smart water dispensers and campus water storage tanks into a single control tower.

---

## 🤖 The 8 Integrated Sub-Agents Controlled by Master AI

1. **Sub-Agent 1 — IoT Telemetry & Anomaly Detector Agent**: Multivariate Isolation Forest anomaly scoring across 20+ sensor channels.
2. **Sub-Agent 2 — XGBoost RUL Predictor Agent**: Remaining Useful Life ($RUL_{days}$) forecasting ($R^2 = 0.9185$).
3. **Sub-Agent 3 — Groq LLaMA 3.3 70B RAG Diagnostic Agent**: LLM technical SOP reasoning & error code manuals.
4. **Sub-Agent 4 — Autonomous Dispenser Work Order Agent**: Priority ticketing, technician scheduling & audit log.
5. **Sub-Agent 5 — Tank Water Quality & Turbidity Agent**: Monitors storage tanks (pH, TDS, Turbidity NTU, Level %).
6. **Sub-Agent 6 — Algae & Sediment Degradation Predictor**: Forecasts algae growth risks and tank flushing due dates.
7. **Sub-Agent 7 — Autonomous Tank Sanitation Booking Agent**: Schedules tank flushing & notifies residence halls.
8. **Sub-Agent 8 — Chemical & Spare Parts Replenishment Agent**: Auto-reorders chlorine sanitizer & UV lamps.
